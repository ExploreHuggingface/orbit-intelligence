Today ORBIT Intelligence opens its doors. If you have ever refreshed a marketplace at midnight, kept a spreadsheet of
"watches to watch", or lost a listing to someone who replied eleven minutes faster — this product is for you.

## The gap we're filling

Search engines are extraordinary at discovery and useless at remembering. CRMs organize but never look. Market
intelligence analyzes everything except *your* specific hunt. ORBIT sits in the gap between them: an operational layer
that turns passive web search into an active opportunity pipeline.

Every interaction produces a structured record — a monitor, an opportunity, a collection, a comparison — not a
conversation transcript. It is a command center, not a chatbot.

## What ships today

- **Monitors** that watch sources on your schedule and score every match
- **Deal scores** that price each listing against an estimated reference band, evidence linked
- **Collections and comparisons** that turn matches into a decision pipeline
- **Alerts** for new matches, price drops and availability changes
- **Market insights** across every category you track

## What we're honest about

This build runs on curated sample data. Live crawling activates with the production backend — and until then, every
seeded record carries a "sample data" badge and every estimate is labeled illustrative. We think a product that
shows you exactly how it works, with the edges clearly marked, beats one that performs confidence.
